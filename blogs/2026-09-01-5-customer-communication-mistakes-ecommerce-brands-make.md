With the summer break coming to an end, many ecommerce businesses are already preparing for the peak shopping season ahead. As marketing campaigns are finalized and operations ramp up, customer communication becomes just as important as inventory and promotions. Peak shopping season is one of the biggest opportunities for ecommerce brands – but it’s also one of the easiest times to lose customers.
