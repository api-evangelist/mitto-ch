Aduna and Mitto announce a new agreement under which Mitto will leverage Aduna’s Open API service to expand its operational capabilities. Through this collaboration, Mitto will incorporate Aduna’s Open APIs into its technology ecosystem to support internal initiatives and enhance flexibility. The addition provides new opportunities to broaden supporting technologies and strengthen operational efficiency.
